To my esteemed friend, Claudius,

I write to you with hands still trembling from the horror we have witnessed. The unthinkable has occurred. Rome, the Eternal City, has been breached. Alaric and his Visigoths entered through the Salarian Gate three days past, though rumors circulate that it was opened from within by treacherous slaves.

The destruction is beyond words. They stripped the mausoleums of Augustus and Hadrian of their treasures. For three days, they pillaged, though I must confess, the barbarians showed strange restraint towards those seeking sanctuary in the basilicas of St. Peter and St. Paul. Still, the smoke from burning villas obscures the sun. 

Our world is unraveling. I fear the empire will never recover from this humiliation.

Yours in sorrow,
Senator Marcus Aurelius (the younger)
August 28, 410 AD
