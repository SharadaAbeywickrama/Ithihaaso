Chronicles of Ataulf, Brother of King Alaric (Excerpt)

When we took the city of the Romans, it was not out of bloodlust, but necessity. They had broken their treaties, denied us land, and starved our people. Alaric gave strict orders: no blood was to be shed unnecessarily, and the holy places of the Christians were to be untouched. 

We took their gold and their wealth, yes, for it was owed to us by right of contract. The Romans weep for their lost statues, but we fought for our survival. We left the city standing after three days, taking only what we could carry. The city was not destroyed, only its pride.
